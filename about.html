About Us

Bull Therapy is a free stock analysis platform built for everyone — whether you're just starting out or have years of experience in the market. We believe that good financial decisions shouldn't require a finance degree. That's why we built a tool that puts professional-grade data directly in your hands, presented in a clear and simple way.

Our Mission

We want to give every investor — beginner or experienced — the clearest possible picture of any stock before they invest. The more informed you are, the better your decisions. We don't make decisions for you. We give you the data, the context, and the analysis so you can make them yourself.

Investing Is Risky — But Risk Can Be Managed

Investing in the stock market always involves risk. Prices go down as well as up, and past performance is never a guarantee of future results. But risk isn't something to fear — it's something to understand and manage. With the right information and the right tools, you can make smarter decisions, reduce unnecessary risk, and improve your odds of long-term success.

That's exactly what Bull Therapy is built for.

What You Get

Search any stock and instantly get a full picture: earnings growth, valuation vs. the sector, financial health, technical momentum, analyst targets, insider activity, news, and more — all in one place, updated daily. No subscriptions. No paywalls. No jargon.

The Score

Our scoring system combines Growth, Valuation, Quality, and Technical signals into a single score from 0 to 100, giving you a clear starting signal: 🟢 Buy (66–100) · 🟡 Wait (41–65) · 🔴 Avoid (0–40). Think of it as a compass, not a GPS — it points you in a direction, but you decide where to go.
