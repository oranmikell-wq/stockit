About Bull Therapy

The market is loud. Everyone has an opinion. Half of them are trying to sell you something. We built Bull Therapy for the investor who wants to cut through the noise and actually understand what they're buying.

Why We Built This

Most retail investors make decisions based on headlines, tips, or gut feeling. Not because they're careless -but because the tools that professional analysts use have always been behind a paywall, buried in spreadsheets, or written in language designed to keep people out.

We thought that was wrong. So we fixed it.

What Bull Therapy Does

We pull real financial data -earnings growth, valuation, margins, insider activity, analyst targets, technical momentum and more -run it through a structured scoring model, and give you a clear, honest picture of any stock in seconds. No subscription. No spin. No agenda.

Just the data you need to make a better decision.

Built for Everyone

Whether you're placing your first trade or managing a portfolio you've built over decades, the fundamentals are the same. A good business at a fair price, with strong momentum and trustworthy management, beats a hot tip every time. Bull Therapy helps you find those businesses -and avoid the ones that only look good on the surface.

On Risk

Investing always involves risk. We won't pretend otherwise. But there's a big difference between informed risk and blind risk. The investors who consistently do well over time aren't the ones who take the biggest swings -they're the ones who do their homework, stay disciplined, and never invest in something they don't understand.

Bull Therapy won't eliminate risk. But it will make sure you're never going in blind.
