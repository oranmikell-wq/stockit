How We Score Stocks

Every stock is evaluated across four categories, each scored 0–100 and combined into a final signal: 🟢 Buy (66–100) / 🟡 Wait (41–65) / 🔴 Avoid (0–40).

Growth - 35%

The largest factor. We analyze EPS surprise, EPS growth, and revenue growth - because companies that consistently grow earnings tend to outperform over time.

Valuation - 25%

A great business at the wrong price is a bad investment. We use PEG ratio, FCF yield, and P/E vs. sector to determine whether the market's expectations are realistic.

Quality - 20%

We filter for businesses with durable advantages: high operating margins, insider ownership, strong return on equity, and healthy balance sheets.

Technical - 20%

Secondary signals used for timing, not conviction. We look at the stock's position relative to its 200-day moving average, distance from all-time highs (ATH), short interest, and RSI.
